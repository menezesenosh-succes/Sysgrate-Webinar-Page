Zoom's collaboration platform — meetings, chat, whiteboard, docs, clips, scheduler, mail, calendar and Zoom Rooms — in one app.

Zoom Workplace is Zoom's collaboration platform — meetings, team chat, whiteboard, docs, clips, scheduler, mail and calendar, plus Zoom Rooms for physical spaces — delivered as one app with AI Companion running across it. Zoom Phone and Zoom Contact Center are separate products that plug into the same platform.

No. Most deployments run alongside Microsoft 365, with SSO and calendar sync keeping it seamless.

No. Most of our deployments run alongside an existing Microsoft 365 estate. Sysgrate is a multi-vendor integrator — we'll tell you honestly if coexistence is the better commercial answer.

AI Companion is included at no extra cost with eligible paid Zoom licences. Available features vary by plan, and it needs enabling and configuring at account level — including your data and retention settings — which is part of every Sysgrate deployment.

Zoom's stated position is that customer content is not used to train Zoom's or third parties' AI models.

Zoom's stated position is that customer content such as audio, video, chat and files is not used to train Zoom's or third parties' AI models. We walk through the current data-handling and admin controls in detail during deployment so your security team can review them against your own policy.

Yes. Sysgrate's Modern workplace and AV practice designs, supplies, installs and supports Zoom Rooms — cameras, audio, displays, scheduling panels and cabling — across India, Singapore, the UAE and Malaysia. Software and room come under one contract.
